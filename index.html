Your Season

When life doesn’t go the way you planned.

Maybe you are young and wondering what the Lord wants you to do with your life. Maybe you are a parent carrying a burden for your family. Maybe your plans fell apart, or you are being asked to let go of something you love.

Mark and I have been in each of those places. Every time, we had to decide again whether we would trust Him.

The real question is not, “Will God ask me to live a life like theirs?” It is, “Will I trust Him with the life He has given me?”

The Book

We never planned to write a book.

For years, friends told us to write our story down. Then one evening on our back deck, Mark wondered out loud how many of the Lord’s mercies we had already forgotten. Not long after, I found boxes of letters in the attic: the letters and ministry reports I had written and saved through all our years of service.

Those letters became this book. It is told mostly through my eyes, with Mark’s thoughts woven in. Some of it is joyful. Some of it was hard to write. All of it points to the same thing: the Lord can be trusted, even when you cannot see what He is doing.

“What follows is not merely our story. It is the story of the Lord’s faithfulness through every season of surrender.”
From Mark’s preface

A Story from Season One

Three eggs.

At language school in Texas, our food was almost gone and the next support check was two weeks away. I asked the Lord for three eggs for breakfast the next morning.

“The next morning, Paula eagerly looked outside the door of our little travel trailer. There, sitting on our patio chair, was an egg carton. For a moment, she could hardly believe what she was seeing. She picked it up, opened it, and called for me to come. Inside were three eggs. Exactly three. Not more, not less. The Lord had answered her prayer with such tenderness and precision that there was no way to explain it away. With tears of rejoicing, we scrambled them and squeezed a little ketchup over them from a single leftover fast-food packet.”

From Chapter 2, “The Calling Forward”

That was not the end of it. Before the day was over, the Lord had given us more food than our little trailer could hold. But the bigger gift was what He was teaching us about depending on Him.

Questions

What is Seasons of Surrender?

+

Seasons of Surrender is a memoir by Mark & Paula Batory: a personal account of marriage, family, missionary service, illness, redirection, leadership, and letting go, told across four seasons: Trust, Trial, Refinement, and Release.

Who are Mark and Paula Batory?

+

Mark and Paula Batory met as teenagers who shared a desire to serve the Lord. They married in 1977 and began missionary service the following year. Their service included Mexico, church planting, ministry among Spanish-speaking people, missions leadership, and care for fellow servants of Christ. Paula is a wife, mother, missionary, and storyteller; Mark is a missionary, church planter, and missions leader.

Meet the Authors

Mark and I met as teenagers who both wanted to serve the Lord. We married in 1977 and began missionary service the next year. Over the years He led us through Mexico, church planting, ministry among Spanish-speaking people, missions leadership, and caring for other missionaries.

Our lives have not been free from hard times, but they have been full of God’s kindness. We are still two people learning what it means to follow Jesus Christ.

Mark & Paula Batory

Who we are

Mark

Missionary, church planter, and missions leader. Mark’s reflections trace the calling, decisions, relationships, and releases through which the Lord has continued to teach him to trust.

Paula

Wife, mother, missionary, and storyteller. For many years Paula wrote the letters and ministry reports that would one day help us remember the Lord’s faithfulness through every season.

The Book

We never planned to write a book.

For years, friends told us to write our story down. Then one evening on our back deck, Mark wondered out loud how many of the Lord’s mercies we had already forgotten. Not long after, I found boxes of letters in the attic: the letters and ministry reports I had written and saved through all our years of service.

Those letters became this book. It is told mostly through my eyes, with Mark’s thoughts woven in. Some of it is joyful. Some of it was hard to write. All of it points to the same thing: the Lord can be trusted, even when you cannot see what He is doing.

“What follows is not merely our story. It is the story of the Lord’s faithfulness through every season of surrender.”
From Mark’s preface

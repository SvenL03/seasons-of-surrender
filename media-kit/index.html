Long description

We all say we trust God until He asks us to surrender.

Seasons of Surrender is the true story of a young couple who answered God’s call, only to learn that surrender is not a single moment, but a lifetime of yielding.

Mark and Paula Batory married in 1977 and left Michigan the next year as young missionaries. Language school in Texas, mountain roads and villages in Mexico, church planting, illness, opposition, the loss of a country they loved, missions leadership, and long seasons of waiting followed. Through nearly fifty years, the Lord taught them that His faithfulness does not depend on their circumstances.

The story is drawn from decades of letters and ministry reports Paula saved. It is told mostly through her eyes, with Mark’s reflections woven in, across four seasons:

Trust: Surrendering the Strength of Our Youth
Trial: Surrendering Our Way in the Wilderness
Refinement: Surrendering to an Unchosen Path
Release: Surrendering What We Cannot Keep

Each chapter is carried by a line from a beloved hymn. The appendix, Truths to Carry Forward, gathers the biblical lessons of every chapter with Scripture (KJV) for personal reflection or group study.

For young people weighing God’s call, believers walking through a hard season, missionaries and pastors facing the seasons of letting go, and churches, small groups, and Bible college classes.

This is not the story of two extraordinary people. It is the testimony of two ordinary people learning, season by season, to place their lives in the hands of an extraordinary God.

The Batorys wrote it for three readers: young people weighing full surrender to missions, believers in the middle of a trial, and faithful servants facing the hard surrender of retirement. They also hope it moves missionaries to keep a record of their own stories.

Main messages, for interviewers and pastors

  1. Surrender is not one moment. It’s a lifetime of yielding, season by season.
  2. God’s faithfulness doesn’t depend on our circumstances.
  3. Every season has its own surrender: the strength of youth, our own way, the path we’d choose, and what we can’t keep.
  4. Keep a record. The letters became the book, and every missionary has a story worth saving.

About the Authors

Mark and Paula Batory

Short bio

For fifty years, Mark and Paula Batory have served the Lord together through missionary work, church planting, leadership, mentoring and missionary care. Seasons of Surrender is their story.

Long bio

For fifty years, Mark and Paula Batory have served the Lord together through missionary work, church planting, leadership, mentoring and missionary care. Their lives continue to be shaped by a shared desire to follow Christ wherever He leads and to encourage others along the way.

Seasons of Surrender is their story, told from Paula’s view with Mark’s voice woven in, and written from forty years of saved letters. It traces four seasons of the Christian life: Trust, Trial, Refinement and Release.

Paula

Paula Batory writes warmly as a wife, mother, missionary and careful witness to God’s faithfulness. For fifty years she has served alongside her husband, Mark, in missionary work, church planting and missionary care. Seasons of Surrender is drawn from the letters she kept across four decades.

Mark

Mark Batory has spent fifty years in missions, church planting, leadership and mentoring. He continues to minister with a burden to strengthen churches, missionaries and men for faithful service to Christ. He is co-author, with his wife, Paula, of Seasons of Surrender.
